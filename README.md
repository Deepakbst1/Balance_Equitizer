  # Balance-Equitizer
  Help you to balance your equitization. Break down the equity into equal parts and calculate how much each person should pay. Built using Next.js and Tailwind CSS, it helps groups manage shared spending and generates polished PDF summaries for easy sharing.

  ## Project need
  Whether you’re traveling with friends, living with roommates, or managing group outings, tracking shared expenses can get messy. Balance-Equitizer helps you stay organized by:
  * Keeping all group expenses in one place.
  * Clearly showing who paid and who owes.
  * Reducing the number of transactions needed to settle up.
  * Supporting multiple payers per expense.
  * Creating professional PDF receipts for transparency and records.

  ## How It Works
  Users add their names as participants.
  They enter the amount they spent on an expense along with a brief description.
  The app calculates the total cost and divides it among all participants based on their contributions.
  Each participant sees how much they owe or are owed after settling up.

  ## Key Features
  Participant Management.
  Add or remove members anytime.
  No limit on the number of users.
  Simple and clutter-free interface.
  
  ## Supports:
  One person paying for everyone.
  Multiple people paying equally.
  Multiple people paying different amounts
  Expense categories like Food, Travel, Stay, etc.
  Clean, receipt-style PDF format.
